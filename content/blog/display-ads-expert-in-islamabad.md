## What display and remarketing actually are

**Display ads** are visual ads, banners, images and short videos, shown on websites, apps, YouTube and Gmail through the Google Display Network, and on Facebook and Instagram through Meta.

**Remarketing** (also called retargeting) means showing those ads specifically to people who already interacted with you. They visited your site, watched your video, engaged with your Instagram or are on your customer list.

Display can also reach new people, but for most Islamabad businesses, remarketing is where it pays best. These people already know your name. You are reminding, not introducing.

## Long decisions need different messages over time

Someone who viewed a property brochure yesterday is in a different place from someone who read a blog post three weeks ago. They should not see the same ad. I build remarketing in layers based on interest level and time.

| Audience | Example for a property developer | Message | Typical window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hot leads who started but did not finish a form | Opened the booking form | "Still interested? Talk to our sales team today" | 1 to 7 days |
| Project page viewers | Viewed the apartment details | Floor plans, payment plan, site visit invite | 7 to 30 days |
| Area or blog readers | Read about living in Gulberg Greens | Construction updates, amenities, testimonials | 30 to 90 days |
| Video viewers | Watched most of the project video on YouTube | Booking offer or site visit weekend | 30 to 90 days |
| Existing buyers | Already booked a unit | Excluded, or shown referral and upgrade messages | Ongoing |

The same idea works for schools during admissions, wedding venues before the season and car dealers during launches.

## Frequency: reminding without annoying

The fastest way to damage a brand with display ads is to follow people around all day with the same banner. In a city where people often talk about ads they find irritating, this matters.

## Placement controls on the Google Display Network

The Google Display Network is huge, which is both its strength and its biggest risk. Left on default settings, campaigns can spend heavily on mobile games and low quality apps, where many clicks are accidental.

For Islamabad clients, I usually:

- Exclude most mobile app categories, especially games, unless they are proven to work.
- Exclude sensitive content categories that do not suit your brand.
- Review placement reports regularly and block poor performing sites.
- Prefer quality news, lifestyle and niche websites your audience actually reads.
- Use conversion based bidding once enough data exists.

Remarketing depends on tracking. I install and check the Google tag, the Meta Pixel and the Conversions API so audiences build properly and results are measured. If you advertise to Europe or the UK, consent rules apply to tracking and remarketing, and I set up consent tools and Google Consent Mode where needed.

## Build audiences before you need them

Remarketing only works if there is someone to remarket to. A common problem I see is a business that wants to launch remarketing for a big sale or admissions season, only to find its audience lists are almost empty because tracking was never set up.

Audience lists build over time as people visit your website, watch your videos and engage with your social profiles. Google and Meta also have minimum list sizes before ads can serve. The practical lesson is simple: install tracking and create your audience lists months before you plan to use them. It costs nothing to collect them, and when your campaign starts, you will have a warm audience waiting.

The most useful lists to create early for Islamabad businesses include:

- All website visitors, kept for as long as the platform allows.
- Visitors to key pages, such as pricing, a project page or an admission form.
- People who started but did not finish a form.
- YouTube viewers who watched most of a video.
- Instagram and Facebook profile engagers.
- Customer lists, uploaded securely where the platform rules allow.

## Example: an admissions season plan

Here is how I might plan remarketing for a hypothetical school or academy in Islamabad ahead of admissions. Details are illustrative.

**Eight weeks before.** Check that the Google tag and Meta Pixel are working and that lists are building. Prepare banners for each stage, with campus photos, key dates and a clear call to action.

**Six weeks before.** Start light remarketing to past website visitors with a "Admissions opening soon" message and a link to register interest.

**Admissions open.** Show stronger messages to people who viewed fee or admission pages: open days, test dates and a direct WhatsApp option. Use YouTube viewers of the campus tour as a separate audience with a visit invitation.

**Final two weeks.** Focus budget on hot audiences, such as form starters and repeat visitors, with deadline reminders. Keep frequency caps in place so reminders stay helpful.

**After the deadline.** Pause or switch messages to waiting list and next intake, and exclude families who already applied.

## Questions to ask a display ads expert

Before you hire anyone for display and remarketing, ask:

1. How will you stop my ads appearing in low quality apps and games?
2. What frequency cap will you use, and why?
3. How will you separate audiences by interest level?
4. How will you exclude people who already bought or enquired?
5. How will you report clicks, conversions and view through conversions?

Clear, specific answers are a good sign. Vague promises about "reach" are not.

## Mistakes I see from Islamabad advertisers

1. **No frequency cap,** so the same people see the same banner dozens of times.
2. **One message for every visitor,** whether they read a blog or nearly booked.
3. **Showing ads to people who already bought.**
4. **Default placements,** with most of the budget going to mobile games and apps.
5. **Old creative running for months.**
6. **Judging display on direct clicks alone,** or the opposite, claiming credit for every view.
