## How people in Islamabad actually search

If you want to win local searches, start with the words people type. In the twin cities, a few patterns show up again and again.

**Sector and area names.** People search "dentist F-8", "gym in E-11", "plumber G-13", "school in Bahria Town Phase 8" or "physiotherapist DHA 2". These are some of the highest intent searches you can buy, because the person has already decided where they want to go.

**"Near me" searches on mobile.** A large share of local searches come from phones, often while someone is already out. These people want a phone number, opening hours and directions, not a long article.

**Roman Urdu and mixed language.** Searches like "ac repair wala islamabad" or "bachon ka doctor F-10" are common. They are often cheaper than the English versions because fewer advertisers target them.

**Landmarks and markets.** Jinnah Super, F-7 Markaz, Centaurus, Giga Mall, Saddar and Commercial Market all appear in searches. If your business is near one of them, mention it.

A good keyword plan for an Islamabad business uses all of these, grouped into tight ad groups so that each ad repeats the exact place and service the person searched for.

## Location targeting: the setting that wastes the most money

One setting causes more wasted spend in Islamabad accounts than any other. In Google Ads location options, you can target people who are **in** your location, or people who are in **or interested in** it.

The default often includes people who are merely interested in Islamabad. That can mean someone in Karachi reading about the capital, or someone abroad researching a visit. For a clinic, a gym or a home service, those clicks are almost always wasted.

For most local businesses, I set targeting to people present in, or regularly in, the target area. Then I build the area itself in layers:

- **A tight radius** around the business, often 3 to 5 km, with higher bids.
- **A wider area** covering the rest of Islamabad and the parts of Rawalpindi you can realistically serve.
- **Exclusions** for areas you never serve, so you do not pay for clicks you cannot convert.

For businesses with branches, such as a restaurant chain or a school system, each branch gets its own location targeting and ads that mention that branch by name.

## Assets that make local ads work harder

Google Ads lets you add extra information to your ads, called assets. For local businesses, three of them matter a lot.

**Call assets** add a phone number or call button. On mobile, people can tap to call without visiting your site. I always track these calls as conversions, so Google learns which keywords bring real phone enquiries.

**Location assets** connect your Google Business Profile, so your ad can show your address, distance and a directions link. If your profile is not verified and complete, fix that first. It also helps your organic Maps ranking.

**Sitelinks and callouts** add useful details such as "Same day appointments", "Female doctor available", "Free home visit in Islamabad" or "Open until 11pm". These help people choose you before they even click.

## Tracking calls and WhatsApp, not just clicks

In Pakistan, a large share of leads arrive by phone or WhatsApp rather than web forms. If your Google Ads account only tracks form submissions, it is blind to most of your results, and Google's automated bidding will optimise in the wrong direction.

For local clients, I usually track:

- Calls from call assets, counted when they last longer than a set number of seconds.
- Clicks on phone numbers on the website.
- Clicks on WhatsApp buttons, tracked as a separate action.
- Form submissions on thank you pages.
- Bookings or sales confirmed later, imported back where possible.

Once tracking is in place, the account can use smart bidding properly and you can see which keywords bring actual customers.

## Search campaigns or Performance Max for a local business?

Google pushes Performance Max hard, and it does have a place. It can show your ads across Search, Maps, YouTube, Display and Gmail from one campaign, using location assets to reach people nearby. For restaurants, showrooms and multi-branch businesses, that reach can be useful.

The catch is control. Performance Max gives you far less visibility into which searches triggered your ads, and it can happily spend on people who were already going to find you, such as those searching your brand name. For a clinic, a lawyer or a home service in Islamabad, where every lead matters and budgets are often modest, I almost always start with standard Search campaigns. They let me see every search term, block waste quickly and put money behind the exact sector and service keywords that convert.

Once Search is profitable and tracking is solid, Performance Max can be tested alongside it with a separate budget. That order protects your money while still leaving room to grow.

## Landing pages that convert local visitors

Even a perfect campaign fails if the page people land on does not help them act. For Islamabad and Rawalpindi businesses, the pages that convert best usually share a few features:

- **The area in the headline.** "Dental Clinic in F-8, Islamabad" reassures people they are in the right place.
- **A tap to call button and a WhatsApp button** visible without scrolling on mobile.
- **Opening hours, address and a map,** especially for clinics and restaurants.
- **Real photos** of your premises and team instead of stock images.
- **Prices or price ranges** where possible. Many local customers will not enquire if they fear the price is hidden.
- **Fast loading on mobile data.** A slow page loses people before they read a word.

I review landing pages as part of every Google Ads project and suggest changes in plain terms, even if your web developer makes them.

## Mistakes I find in most Islamabad accounts

When I audit local accounts, the same problems appear almost every time.

1. **Location set to "interest" instead of presence,** paying for clicks from other cities.
2. **One ad group for everything,** so a search for "root canal F-8" sees a generic "Best Dental Clinic" ad.
3. **Broad match keywords without negatives,** matching unrelated searches.
4. **No call tracking** for a business that gets most enquiries by phone.
5. **Ads running at night** when nobody can answer the phone.
6. **Sending every click to the homepage** instead of a page about the specific service.
7. **Accepting every automatic recommendation,** many of which simply raise spend.

Fixing these usually brings a clear drop in cost per lead within the first month.
